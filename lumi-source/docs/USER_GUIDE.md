# Lumi: one-page user guide

**Lumi helps you feel good on and off your screen.** It is private, kind, and not a therapist.

- **Help (top-right, every screen):** emergency number, a helpline, your trusted person, an anonymous note to a helper, breathing and grounding.
- **Today:** a thought for the day based on your goals, a 10-second mood check-in, screen time, your next phone-free time, your streak, an idea for something to do instead of scrolling.
- **Habits:** screen-time charts (Android: automatic; other phones: quick check-in), app limits with a calm *pause and think* screen, phone-free times (study, sleep, meals, prayer, personal), bedtime mode, focus timer and grayscale, gaming timer with a "last round" warning and weekly budget.
- **Mind:** mood tracking, gratitude and wins journal, worry jar, breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, sleep log, reality-check cards, "how do I feel after scrolling?".
- **Connect:** trusted person, anonymous note to a helper, moderated anonymous peer-support space, events and clubs, connection ideas, online safety guides (blocking/reporting, image threats, consent, privacy check-up), private evidence log with PDF export.
- **Me:** weekly reflection (only you see it), real-life goals, streaks with kind restarts, challenges, badges, settings.

**Your data:** stays on your phone unless you join the community features. Settings → Privacy → export everything or delete everything at any time. App lock (fingerprint/PIN) in Settings.

**Android full app (APK):** allow *Usage access* and *Display over other apps* in Habits → App limits to turn on pause screens. Add the Lumi quote widget from your home screen's widget menu.

**Try it without your own data:** Welcome screen → *Try the demo*, or Settings → Demo mode.

If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. Lumi cannot respond in a crisis.
