# Lumi: one-page user guide

**Lumi helps you feel good on and off your screen.** It is private, kind, and not a therapist.

- **Help (top-right, every screen):** emergency number, a helpline, your trusted person, an anonymous note to a helper, breathing and grounding.
- **Today:** a thought for the day based on your goals, one-tap quick actions (check in, focus, eye break, breathe), a 10-second mood check-in, today's body goals, your crew challenge, screen time, your next phone-free time, your streak, an idea for something to do instead of scrolling.
- **Habits:** screen-time charts (Android: automatic; other phones: quick check-in), app limits with a calm *pause and think* screen, phone-free times (study, sleep, meals, prayer, personal), bedtime mode, focus timer with *gentle* or *deep focus* (blocks every app except calls, messages and the ones you allow) and an optional *locked session*, grayscale, gaming timer with a "last round" warning and weekly budget.
- **Strict mode (Habits → Strict mode):** hides *Open anyway* once a limit, phone-free time, bedtime or focus session applies; a *scrolling session cap* locks a limited app after one long session (e.g. 20 min, then a 30-minute cool-down); a *commitment lock* (1, 3 or 7 days) means rules can be tightened but not loosened. Emergency unlock takes a 15-minute wait. Calls, messages, Settings and Lumi are never blocked.
- **Eyes and body (Habits → Eyes and body):** guided 20-20-20 eye breaks, a 3-minute stretch routine, and daily goals for water, movement, stretch breaks, eye breaks and time outside, with a 7-day chart. Reminders for eye breaks, movement and water within the hours you choose. On Android with usage access, eye breaks follow your real continuous screen time.
- **Crew challenges (Habits → Crew challenges):** real-world habit games with family or friends, fully offline. Pick habits (phone-free dinner, a walk, reading…), add people, choose a *friendly contest* or a *team goal* and a prize. Points, perfect-day bonuses, levels, streaks and a leaderboard. Pass one phone around, or share a code by message; swapping codes later merges everyone's check-ins.
- **Mind:** mood tracking, gratitude and wins journal, worry jar, breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, sleep log, reality-check cards, "how do I feel after scrolling?".
- **Connect:** trusted person, anonymous note to a helper, moderated anonymous peer-support space, events and clubs, connection ideas, online safety guides (blocking/reporting, image threats, consent, privacy check-up), private evidence log with PDF export.
- **Me:** weekly reflection (only you see it), real-life goals, streaks with kind restarts, challenges, badges, settings.

**Your data:** stays on your phone unless you join the community features. Settings → Privacy → export everything or delete everything at any time. App lock (fingerprint/PIN) in Settings.

**Android full app (APK):** allow *Usage access* and *Display over other apps* in Habits → App limits to turn on pause screens. Add the Lumi quote widget from your home screen's widget menu.

**Try it without your own data:** Welcome screen → *Try the demo*, or Settings → Demo mode.

If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. Lumi cannot respond in a crisis.
